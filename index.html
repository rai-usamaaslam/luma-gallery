ABOUT LUMA

Made to be
lived with.

We started Luma because most lighting is an afterthought — a fixture chosen last, bright enough to see by and nothing more. We wanted to make light itself worth noticing.

Every piece is shaped by hand from natural materials and tested in real rooms, at real hours, before it's allowed to leave the studio.
